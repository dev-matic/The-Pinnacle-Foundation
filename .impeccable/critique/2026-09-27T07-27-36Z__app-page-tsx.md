## Design Specificity Verdict
Mostly authored. Weak spots are the choreography, which leans on the standard award-site kit (cursor-follow image list, outline words filling on scroll, horizontal numbered track, count-up stats, image-scales-to-full-bleed), and copy that says "Ghana" nine times without naming a place, date or quantity. ## Priority Issues
1. [P1] Homepage too long and too scroll-captured for the actions it exists to drive. ~26,000px; four pinned scenes (ui-ux-pro-max: pin at most 1–2 per page); Give/Serve/Partner arrives around screen 22 of 29 (27 of 31 on mobile). Fix: fold OpeningWords into the statement, replace the Journey and Transparency blocks on Home with short link strips, shorten remaining pins, move Pathways up; target under ~16 screens. Commands: distill, layout.
2. [P1] Donate expectation gap and trust valley right before the ask. Six Donate prompts lead to a page where online giving "opens soon"; all five transparency documents read "Publishing soon" just before the final CTA; "Support Projects Like This" silently opens the contact form. Fix: honest CTA labels, collapse the empty document list into one committed sentence until a document is live, lead with the one verifiable fact (registration), unify CTA labels. Command: clarify.
3. [P1] Placeholders deflate the full-bleed moments. Hero expansion, featured project and final CTA become full screens of hatched beige; on mobile the caption sits behind the featured-project CTA. Fix: a full-bleed "scene" variant of the placeholder (darker surface, large caption), or cap expansion until photography arrives; photography brief for landscape full-bleed with focal point inside the arch. Commands: harden, polish.
4. [P2] Accessibility and touch edges. Muted text on terracotta 3.6:1 and olive 4.4:1 (below 4.5:1); work summaries hover-only; Tab reaches the invisible nav during the intro; buttons show no pointer cursor (Tailwind v4 default); footer links 20–24px tall and filter chips 38px on mobile (below 44px); mobile hero shows "ACTIO" behind the arch; "Compassion" step label collides with the arch at 1440px. Commands: adapt, audit.
5. [P2] Every headline shouts and the slogan repeats. About ten uppercase giant heads; three back-to-back value-word sequences (statement, Faith/Love/Service/Hope, Faith/Compassion/Action). Fix: reserve giant display type for hero, featured project and final CTA; set other heads smaller or in the serif; replace at least three slogans with concrete sentences. Commands: typeset, quieter, clarify.
